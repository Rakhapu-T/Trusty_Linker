# 1. Simple Tuna Rice Bowl

### Ingredients

- 1 cup **cooked rice**
- 1 can **tuna** (drained)
- 1–2 tbsp **mayonnaise** (optional but tasty)
- 1 tbsp **soy sauce**
- ½ tsp **black pepper**
- Optional extras (if you have them):
    - chopped **onion**
    - **egg**
    - **spring onion**
    - **chilli flakes**
    - **lemon juice**

### Instructions
1. **Cook rice** (or use leftover rice).
2. **Drain the tuna** and break it apart with a fork.
3. Mix tuna with **mayo, soy sauce, and pepper** in a bowl.
4. Put the **hot rice** in a bowl and add the tuna mixture on top.
5. Add any extras you like (onion, chilli flakes, etc.).
6. Mix everything together and eat.


# 2. High-Calorie Tuna Rice Bowl
### Ingredients
- 1½–2 cups **cooked rice**
- 1 can **tuna** (in oil if possible)
- 1 **egg**
- 2 tbsp **mayonnaise**
- 1 tbsp **olive oil** or **butter**
- 1 tbsp **soy sauce**
- ½ **avocado** (optional but great for calories)
- Black pepper or chilli flakes

### Instructions
1. Heat a pan and add **olive oil or butter**.
2. **Scramble the egg** in the pan.
3. Add the **rice** and fry it for 2–3 minutes.
4. Add the **tuna** and mix.
5. Add **soy sauce** and **pepper**.
6. Turn off heat and mix in **mayonnaise**.
7. Top with **avocado slices**.
